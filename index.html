Privacy policy for Low Pass

App: Low Pass (package com.kirillom.planes).

The game needs an internet connection before a flight can start. It does not create an account.

What stays on the device

The phone stores the pilot name, campaign progress, planes, upgrades, language setting, and which in-app purchases have been made. This data is not sent to a server run by the developer.

Ads

Ads are shown through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource). The ad network may receive the device advertising ID, IP address, and information about the ad that was shown, so it can choose and record ads. Buying “no ads” turns off the full-screen ad between levels. The shop video that grants coins still plays.

Purchases

Paid items (no ads, gems, planes, and the battle pass) are handled by Google Play. Google collects and stores the payment details, not the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

For questions about this data, use the email address shown on the app’s Google Play page.
