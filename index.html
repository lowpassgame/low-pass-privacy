Privacy Policy for Low Pass

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Low Pass (package com.kirillom.planes) collects, uses, and shares information about you when you play our mobile game. The game needs an internet connection before a flight can start. It does not create an account.

2. Data

The phone stores the pilot name, campaign progress, planes, upgrades, language setting, and which in-app purchases have been made. This data stays on the device and is not sent to a server run by the developer.

Through third-party services (Unity LevelPlay and Google Play), the following may be collected:

  • Device information (model, OS version).
  • Unique device identifiers (advertising ID, such as AAID).
  • Approximate location (country or city) based on the IP address, used by the ad network.

3. In-App Purchases

Our game includes virtual goods (no ads, gems, planes, and the battle pass). All financial transactions are processed by Google Play. We do not store or have access to your credit card information or billing address.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following services that may collect data:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource): for displaying ads. The ad network may receive the advertising ID, IP address, and information about the ad that was shown. Buying “no ads” turns off the full-screen ad between levels. The shop video that grants coins still plays.
  • Google Play: for billing.

5. GDPR and EU User Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact us at lowpassgame@gmail.com to make a request. You can also reset your Advertising ID in your Android device settings.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our game is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Data Deletion

Since our game does not use accounts, we do not store personal profiles. Progress on the device is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. If you wish to request the deletion of any data collected by our third-party partners (Unity LevelPlay or Google), please contact us at lowpassgame@gmail.com. You can also reset your Advertising ID in your Android device settings to clear your ad profile.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions, contact us at: lowpassgame@gmail.com.
